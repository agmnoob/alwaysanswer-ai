FAQ

Questions shops ask before they switch

Will callers know it's an AI?
It identifies as your virtual assistant right away — no deception, just a fast, friendly answer. Most callers care that they got helped instantly and booked.
What if it's an emergency?
You set the rules. The agent can escalate urgent calls to a live number or priority-text you immediately based on keywords you choose (e.g., "gas leak," "no heat," "flooding").
Does it work with my current phone number?
Yes. We can port your existing number or assign a new local one. No change to how customers reach you.
How long to set up?
Most shops are live within a day. We train the agent on your services and area, connect your CRM, then route your calls.
What languages does it support?
English, Spanish, Tagalog, Chinese, Korean, Armenian, and many more. The AI detects the caller's language automatically and responds fluently — no configuration needed. Retell AI supports 10+ languages out of the box.
What CRM do you integrate with?
ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, Mindbody, Square, HubSpot, Close, Pipedrive, and more. Custom webhooks available.
What if the AI can't handle something?
It transfers to your designated backup (you, your manager, on-call tech) with full context — transcript, customer info, CRM link. Zero dropped balls.
Is there a contract?
Month-to-month. Cancel anytime. Trial period with no charge if it's not a fit.
